## Descricao curta

Industrial, high-contrast Plasma 6 theme system for the NODE desktop, with a technical dark flagship and a restrained corporate light variant.

## Descricao longa

NODE Control is the Plasma 6 desktop language used by the NODE client runtime. It combines a precise technical accent blue, restrained industrial surfaces, high legibility and coherent wallpapers for dark and light deployments. The package includes Global Themes, Plasma Styles, Color Schemes and wallpapers designed to match the visual direction already used by the NODE boot and login surfaces.
